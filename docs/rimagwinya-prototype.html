Ri-magwinyaInteractive prototype

A clickable simulation of both roles. Student and staff share one live dataset. Place an order as a student and it appears in the staff queue; mark it ready and the student is notified.

Demo accounts — tap to fill

Try this

  1. Sign in as the student and open Vetkoek — add polony and achar as fillings.
  2. Add fried chips and pick a size, then a drink.
  3. Choose a collection slot and place the order. Note the 4-digit code.
  4. Hit Switch role to jump to staff.
  5. Find the order, start preparing, then mark ready.
  6. Switch back — the push and the status have updated.
  7. As staff, drop an item's stock to zero and watch it sell out on the menu.
