# A student's day

Back to [[Ri-magwinya]]

The whole thing from the student's side, start to finish.

## The night before (or whenever)

You walk past the tuckshop, tap your bank card on the speed point, and tell
the person behind the counter you want fifty rand on your account. They find
you by student number in the app and load it. Your wallet now says R50.00.

That's the only time money physically moves. See [[The campus wallet]].

## Second period, bored

You open the app. It already knows who you are — you signed in weeks ago and
it stays signed in. If you switched on biometric unlock, it asks for your
fingerprint first.

The menu opens on a navy header with your name, your balance, and a bell for
notifications. Under that there's a thin line of weather: something like
*14°C · Light rain · Hot drinks first today*. Because it's cold, the teas and
coffees have moved to the top of the list. See [[Weather]].

You search, or tap a category chip — Meals, Snacks, Drinks. Every row shows
the price and a little pill: **In stock**, **4 left**, or **Sold out**. Sold
out rows are greyed and won't open. See [[The menu]].

## Building the order

You tap **Vetkoek**. A sheet slides up from the bottom.

It doesn't say "R3.00, add to cart". It says *Vetkoeks* with a stepper, and
under that a list of fillings, each with its own stepper. You set two
vetkoeks, two polony, one cheese slice. The button at the bottom keeps
recalculating: **Add to cart · R17.00**.

You could also set the vetkoek count to zero and just buy a snoek filling on
its own. The app will let you, and it won't take a vetkoek off the stock
count. See [[Building an order]].

Then a tea. Different sheet: pick a brand, milk or black, how much sugar.
None of that changes the price — tea is R10 whatever you do to it.

## Checkout

Cart tab. Two lines, with their full descriptions underneath —
*2 vetkoeks, 2 Polony, Cheese slice* — steppers to change quantities, and a
total.

Then you pick a break:

- First break · 09:40–10:00 · **12 of 40 taken**
- Second break · 11:00–11:20 · **38 of 40 taken**
- Afternoon · 14:30–14:50 · 3 of 25 taken

Second break is nearly full. Pick it late enough and it's gone. See
[[Collection slots]].

Payment is your wallet by default. If your balance is too low the wallet
option is blocked with a plain message, and you can choose **pay at the
counter** instead — unless you've already no-showed twice, in which case
that's blocked too and the app tells you why. See [[Pay at the counter]].

Tap **Place order**.

## Waiting

You land on the order screen. Four big tiles across the top with your code in
them — **4 7 2 9** — and a progress rail down the page:

- **Placed** · 09:12
- Preparing
- Ready

R17.00 has left your wallet already. The vetkoek stock dropped by two. The
09:40 slot went from 12 to 13.

You can still cancel at this point and get the money straight back. Once
staff hit "preparing", the cancel button disappears — they've already started
making it. See [[Order statuses]].

You go back to class. The menu now has a little card at the top showing the
order is live.

## The push

At 09:31 the phone buzzes: **Order #1047 is ready for collection.** You tap
it and the app opens straight onto that order. The rail has filled in and the
code tiles are still sitting there. See [[Notifications]].

## The counter

You walk up, say "four seven two nine". Staff find it on their queue, check
the code matches, hand over the bag, and tap **Collected**.

There's no "I've collected it" button on your phone. Only staff can close an
order — otherwise people would mark their own food collected and argue about
it later.

Your loyalty count ticks up. Seven of ten. See [[Loyalty stamps]].

## If it goes wrong

- **Someone bought the last vetkoek while you were in the cart** — the order
  fails with a clear message saying what's out of stock, and nothing is
  charged.
- **The slot filled up** — same, it says so and asks you to pick another.
- **You had no signal** — the order sits as *Waiting for signal. We'll send
  it as soon as you're back online*, and goes the moment you reconnect. See
  [[Offline mode]].
- **You never came** — staff mark it a no-show. It counts against you, and
  two of them means you lose the pay-at-counter option.
