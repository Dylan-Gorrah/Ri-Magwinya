# A staff member's day

Back to [[Ri-magwinya]]

Same day, other side of the counter.

## Opening up

Sign in, land on the **Queue**. Not the menu — the queue is the staff home
screen, because it's the screen they'll stare at all day.

At the top, counters: how many new, how many being prepared, how many sitting
ready. Filter chips under that to narrow it down.

## Orders land by themselves

Nobody refreshes anything. A student places an order and the card appears on
the staff phone, live. Each card carries:

- The order number and the 4-digit code
- The student's name
- Every item with its full description — *2 vetkoeks, 2 Polony, Cheese slice*,
  not just "Vetkoek x1"
- The collection slot and the total

One button per card, and it always says the next thing to do:

- New order → **Start preparing**
- Preparing → **Mark ready**
- Ready → **Collected**

Tapping **Mark ready** pushes a notification to that student's phone. See
[[The staff queue]].

## Checking codes

When someone walks up and says four digits, staff find that card, check the
number matches, hand over the food, tap **Collected**. That's the whole
handover. No signature, no ID.

The code is only unique among today's open orders, which is all it needs to
be. See [[The collection code]].

## No-shows

If the afternoon slot has ended and there's still an order sitting on
**Ready**, a no-show action appears on that card. Tapping it records that the
student didn't come.

Two no-shows and that student can't use pay-at-counter any more. They can
still order — they just have to have money in the wallet first, so the
tuckshop isn't making food on credit for someone who doesn't turn up. See
[[Pay at the counter]].

## Stock

The **Stock** tab is the stock count for every item with a stepper next to it.
Sold five boerewors rolls off the counter to someone who didn't use the app?
Tap it down five. Delivery arrived? Tap it up.

Anything at or below its reorder level shows in a low-stock banner at the top.
Anything at zero goes sold out and disappears from students' ordering
immediately.

This is also where items get added, edited and removed — name, price,
description, category, icon. Prices are data, not code, so a price change is
a thirty-second job on this screen. See [[Stock]].

## Sales

The **Sales** tab, usually at the end of the day:

- Revenue, order count, average order value
- Orders by hour as a bar chart, with the peak called out in words as well as
  colour
- Top sellers
- A CSV export through the normal Android share sheet, so it can go to
  WhatsApp or email

There's also a card for tomorrow's weather with a stock hint — *Cold tomorrow.
Check tea and coffee stock.* See [[Sales]].

## Topping up wallets

A student comes to the counter wanting to load money. They tap their card on
the speed point — that's the bank's machine, nothing to do with the app —
and once it approves, staff open **Top up** from the queue's top bar.

Type the student number, the app finds them, tap a preset (R20, R50, R100,
R200) or type a custom amount between R10 and R1000, confirm. The new balance
shows straight away.

The app never sees a card number. It's a ledger, not a payment processor. See
[[Wallet top-up]].
